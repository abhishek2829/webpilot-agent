# Demo Video Script: Automated Competitor Research

**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Thumbnail text:** "AI Competitor Research" / "Research 10 Companies in 5 Min"

---

## [0:00 - 0:05] HOOK

**Narration:** "I researched 10 competitors in 5 minutes. Here's how."

## [0:05 - 0:12] THE PROBLEM

*Screen: Multiple browser tabs open — competitor websites*

**Narration:** "Manual competitor research means visiting each site, copying pricing, noting features, checking tech stacks. For 10 competitors, that's half a day."

## [0:12 - 0:20] THE COMMAND

*Screen: Terminal*

```
webpilot run competitor-analysis --var competitor_url=https://clerk.com
```

**Narration:** "WebPilot's Research Agent does it for you. Point it at a competitor's website."

## [0:20 - 0:45] THE DEMO

*Screen: Browser opens, navigates to competitor site*

**Narration:** "It navigates to their site, extracts the company name, pricing tiers, key features..."

*Screen: Browser scrolls through pricing page*

**Narration:** "...reads their pricing page, identifies their tech stack..."

*Screen: Checkpoint appears*

**Narration:** "Pauses for your review before extracting anything sensitive."

*Screen: Terminal shows extracted data*

```
COMPETITOR_NAME = Clerk
PRICING_INFO = Free up to 10k MAU, Pro $25/mo
KEY_FEATURES = SSO, MFA, user management, webhooks
```

**Narration:** "Structured data, ready to drop into a spreadsheet."

## [0:45 - 1:00] CTA

**Narration:** "Run it on 10 competitors. Get a full landscape in minutes. WebPilot Agent — link in description."
