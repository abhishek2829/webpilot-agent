## Post 1: Announcement Post

**I spent 6 months building an AI agent that automates the work developers hate most.**

Every time I started a new project, I'd spend 2 hours clicking through dashboards — Clerk, Supabase, Vercel, Stripe, GitHub — copying API keys, configuring settings, doing the same steps I did last month.

So I built WebPilot Agent.

It opens a real browser, navigates the dashboards, clicks the buttons, fills the forms, and extracts the API keys. When it needs my approval (before creating an account or spending money), it pauses and asks.

It started as a DevOps tool, but I realized the same pattern works everywhere:
- Research: competitor analysis, lead research
- Sales: LinkedIn outreach, CRM data entry
- Marketing: social posting, SEO audits
- Finance: invoice tracking, revenue dashboards

Now it's a platform with 6 specialized agents and 26 automation workflows.

It's open source. If you've ever wasted time on repetitive browser tasks, I'd love your feedback.

## Post 2: Thought Leadership

**The future of DevOps isn't more YAML. It's autonomous agents.**

We've been writing infrastructure-as-code for years. Terraform, Pulumi, CloudFormation. Great tools. But they only work with services that have APIs.

Here's the problem: half of the SaaS tools developers use daily have dashboards but no programmatic setup API. Clerk, Vercel's advanced settings, Stripe's webhook configuration — you HAVE to click through a UI.

That's why I believe the next evolution is browser-native agents. Software that can navigate any web interface, understand what's on screen, and take actions — with human approval at critical moments.

I've been building this for the past 6 months. The key insight: Claude's vision API can look at a screenshot and identify UI elements even when selectors change. Combined with Playwright for browser control, you get automation that works with ANY website, not just ones with APIs.

The implication for DevOps teams: developer onboarding that takes 15 minutes instead of a full day. Standardized environment setup that never misses a step.

Would love to hear from DevOps teams dealing with this problem. What tools do you wish you could automate but can't because there's no API?

## Post 3: Sales Angle

**Your sales team spends 60% of their time on non-selling activities.**

Research the company. Find the decision maker on LinkedIn. Send a connection request. Switch to HubSpot. Create a contact. Add notes. Repeat.

The average SDR spends 25+ hours a week on manual data entry and research. That's 25 hours NOT having conversations with prospects.

I built an AI sales pipeline that automates the repetitive parts:

1. Research Agent visits the company website, extracts company info, identifies key people
2. Sales Agent finds the right person on LinkedIn, sends a personalized connection request (after human approval)
3. Sales Agent creates a CRM contact with all research data pre-filled

The pipeline takes 4 minutes per prospect. Manual process: 20-30 minutes.

The critical difference from other automation tools: human-in-the-loop. The agent ALWAYS pauses before sending a message or creating a record. You review, approve, and it continues.

No "oops, it sent 500 wrong messages" incidents.

It's open source and built with Python + Playwright + Claude AI. Happy to share more details with anyone dealing with this problem.
